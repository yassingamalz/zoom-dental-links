ZOOM DENTAL CLINIC — A5 FLYER v6 — PRINTER SPECIFICATION
=====================================================

TRIM        148 x 210 mm (A5), portrait
BLEED       3 mm all round  -> artwork 154 x 216 mm
PRESS PAGE  164 x 226 mm (includes crop-mark margin)
RESOLUTION  vector text + 300 dpi minimum on all placed images
COLOUR      CMYK, no spot plates required

FILES

  ZoomDental_Flyer_A5_v6_bleed_CMYK.pdf             bleed only, no marks

  ZoomDental_Flyer_A5_v6_300dpi.png                 raster proof

BRAND TEAL
  HEX          #0A7183
  CMYK build   C 88  M 43  Y 39  K 10
  Pantone ref  7715 C  (alt: 3155 C)
  If matching to Pantone, 7715 C takes priority over the CMYK build.

INK
  Total area coverage (TAC) peak    256%   — within a 300% limit
  No area exceeds 300%. No rich black anywhere in the artwork.
  Crop marks are 100% K only (single plate) — do not convert to rich black.

QR CODE
  Target        https://zoom-dental-links.zoomdentalcliniccairo.workers.dev/?src=flyer
  Error corr.   M
  Printed size  24.7 mm  ->  module 0.433 mm
  Quiet zone    4 modules, built into the artwork
  Printed on a white panel. Do NOT scale below 24 mm or the modules
  drop under the reliable print floor. Please proof-scan one sheet
  off the press before the full run.

FONTS
  Cairo (Regular / Medium / SemiBold / Bold / Black) — Arabic
  Montserrat (Medium / SemiBold) — Latin
  All embedded and subsetted. No font files needed at your end.

NOTES
  - Background is pure white paper — no flat tint to print.
  - The teal wedge (top-left) and the teal band (bottom) both run to
    the bleed edge. Do not scale artwork to fit.

CHANGES IN v2 (vs v1)
  - Full logo lockup restored: ZOOM / DENTAL CLINIC / rule / Arabic tagline
    Wordmark set in Questrial (closest match to the original letterforms:
    shortened M vertex, circular O, flat Z terminals).
  - Step arrows changed from thin stroked chevrons to solid filled arrows.
  - Step headings Cairo Bold -> ExtraBold; descriptions Regular -> SemiBold.
  - Sparkle marks corrected: were stretched to aspect 2.01 against a source
    aspect of 1.23. Now placed uniformly at 98 x 80 px.
  - Arabic tagline is placed as high-resolution art, not live type (Chromium
    mis-shapes that specific string inside the scaled stage). Effective
    resolution on the trim exceeds 1900 dpi. All other text remains live vector.
